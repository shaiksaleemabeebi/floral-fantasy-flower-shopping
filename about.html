About Floral Fantasy

We're a small team of florists who believe every bouquet should feel personal. Since opening our doors, we've hand-picked, arranged, and delivered fresh flowers for birthdays, weddings, and every "just because" moment in between. Every stem is sourced fresh and every arrangement is made to order — never sitting in a cooler waiting to be sold.

Fresh Sourcing

Flowers are cut and delivered within 24-48 hours, sourced from trusted local growers.

Hand-Crafted

Every bouquet is arranged by hand by our florists, never assembled by machine.

Fast Delivery

Same-day and scheduled delivery options so your flowers arrive exactly when you need them.
